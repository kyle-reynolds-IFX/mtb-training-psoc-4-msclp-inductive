# PSOC&trade; 4 Inductive sensing (ISX) training
This training introduces Inductive sensing (ISX) on the 5th generation Multi-Sense Converter with Low-Power (MSCLP) available on the PSOC&trade; 4000T and PSOC&trade; 4100T Plus. It covers applications that are made possible with ISX, advantages of fly-back sensing with ISX, sensor design and mechanical construction, how to improve the performance and reliability of ISX sensors, the CY8CPROTO-040T-MS kit, and manual and SmartSense&trade; tuning procedures. 


## Device family
- [PSOC&trade; 4](https://www.infineon.com/products/microcontroller/32-bit-psoc-arm-cortex/psoc-4-mcu)

## Agenda
1. Introduction to Infineon's Inductive sensing (ISX)
2. Advantages of fly-back based inductive sensing
3. Sensor design and mechanical construction
4. Performance and reliability 
5. CY8CPROTO-040T-MS kit details
6. Lab 1: Manual inductive sensor tuning process
7. Lab 2: SmartSense&trade; inductive sensor tuning process

## Expected outcome
- Become familiar with Inductive sensing (ISX) on the 5th generation MSCLP block
- Understand advantages of the fly-back based inductive sensing
- Learn about sensor design, mechanical construction, and how to make reliable inductive sensors
- Become proficient with the Inductive sensor (ISX) tuning procedures
